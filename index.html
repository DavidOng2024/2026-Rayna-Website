About the program

Dance, connect, and enjoy the evening

Let's Dance brings together residents who want to learn, practice, and enjoy partner dancing in a supportive and joyful environment. Whether you are just beginning or already love to dance, you will feel right at home.

The program combines beginner instruction, social dance time, and a strong community spirit that makes each night feel special and inviting.
